# Realistic ambient artwork

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. All five final assets are stored in `src/assets/ambient/` as alpha-preserving WebP files. They are original generated illustrations, not documentary photographs of an actual location. The original PNGs remain in the image tool's generated-images directory. Astro generates responsive website derivatives at build time.

## rooftops

Final asset: `src/assets/ambient/rooftops.webp`

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original website footer panorama, isolated cutout with genuinely transparent sky. Create a wide 3:1 landscape matte painting with photographic realism of a lived-in residential rooftop neighborhood in Dhaka, Bangladesh, viewed across rooftops. Weathered warm-gray concrete parapets, black cylindrical water tanks toward the left, terracotta potted jasmine and vines at the far edges, smaller hazy buildings receding in the center, a modest rooftop stairwell room at the right with ONE simple rectangular wooden window glowing warm amber. The window at approximately 82% of image width and 64% of image height is the focus for a later glow overlay. A small laundry line near the middle. Calm blue-hour lighting with realistic textured concrete, natural muted foliage, subtle warm windows, detailed restrained cinematic atmosphere. Highest foreground structures at left and right, lower skyline center. Buildings and plants occupy bottom two-thirds, upper third is actual alpha transparency, with the sky and all area above the uneven skyline cut out completely. Image extends to bottom edge and both side edges. No moon, no stars, no flying objects, no people, no typography, no watermark, no border. This is a unique scene for Rashedul Islam's portfolio, not a copy of any existing website artwork. Prioritize realistic material detail and depth, no flat vectors, no cartoon outlines.
